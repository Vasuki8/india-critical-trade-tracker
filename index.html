01

The national picture

National totals cover India’s reported merchandise imports and exports across the full goods basket. Commodity and partner shares use these official national totals as their denominator. Services are outside this dataset.

02

Chapters & partners

Commodity chapters group goods at the two-digit Harmonized System level. Partner tables retain the country names and any special categories reported by the source. Source rounding can leave small differences between the sum of rows and national totals.

03

Values & time periods

Trade values are shown in US dollars: K means thousand, M means million, and B means billion. Balance is exports minus imports. YoY compares the same month a year earlier; YTD starts in January. The history chart ends at your selected reporting month. Partial years are labeled in the annual tables.

04

The critical watchlist

Critical commodities is a separate, focused view of strategically important groups. Its portfolio totals cover only that watchlist. Overlapping HS headings are counted once in those totals, so adding individual cards can produce a different total. Its latest reporting month is shown separately.

05

Dependency, explained

The critical-watchlist dependency score combines net-import reliance (60%), top supplier share (25%), and supplier concentration, measured by HHI (15%). It is a transparent ranking signal for this watchlist, not an official risk assessment.

06

Quantities with context

Physical quantities in the critical watchlist appear only for validated HS8 mappings. Compatible mass units can be normalized; incompatible units are kept separate. Implied unit values are calculated from trade value and quantity and are not market price quotes.

Traceable to the observation

Source references, reporting periods, HS mappings, retrieval timestamps, and revisions are preserved with the data.
