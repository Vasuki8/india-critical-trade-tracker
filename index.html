01

The watched universe

This tracker follows strategically important commodity groups. It does not represent all of India's trade. Overlapping HS headings are counted once in portfolio totals, so adding individual commodity cards can produce a different total.

02

Values & time periods

Trade values are shown in US dollars: K means thousand, M means million, and B means billion. Balance is exports minus imports. YoY compares the same month a year earlier; YTD starts in January. Partial years are labeled in the annual tables.

03

Dependency, explained

The dependency score combines net-import reliance (60%), top supplier share (25%), and supplier concentration, measured by HHI (15%). It is a transparent ranking signal for this watchlist, not an official risk assessment.

04

Quantities with context

Physical quantities appear only for validated HS8 mappings. Compatible mass units can be normalized; incompatible units are kept separate. Implied unit values are calculated from trade value and quantity and are not market price quotes.

Traceable to the observation

Source references, reporting periods, HS mappings, retrieval timestamps, and revisions are preserved with the data.
